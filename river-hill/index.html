01 / The stay

What you get.

River Hill is booked as a whole property for your group. Here is what is included.

01

Pool deck

Kids' swimming pool, 6 m by 2 m, with a timber deck. Up to 5 in the pool at a time.

Cottages in the trees

Four rooms, AC or non-AC, each with a balcony and an attached bathroom.

Loft cottage

A loft-style cottage with a sleeping platform above the living area.

A deck on the rock with views over the hills. Good for photos.
